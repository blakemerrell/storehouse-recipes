The Week
Open any recipe under Recipes and assign it to a day. The shopping list builds itself from whatever lands here, and a week stays put until you change it — there are no dates, so Monday is just Monday. Keep as many weeks as you like and switch between them above; each one carries its own shopping list. Sharing with someone? Switching the week moves every phone at once.

Pantry
What you keep in. Every recipe asks this list what you would have to go out for, so the line at the foot of a recipe and the shopping list both follow it. Out of the box it is the standard storehouse order — take things off as you stop keeping them, or add what you keep that the books never mention. Anything you take off drops to the foot of the list, so you can put it back.
