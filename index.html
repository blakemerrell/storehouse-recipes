Pantry
This is what the storehouse carried when the books were written. If yours has stopped stocking something, take it off — the recipes stay exactly as they are, and the app simply starts telling you to pick that item up at the grocery store instead. The line at the foot of every recipe and the whole shopping list follow this list. You can also add anything you keep that the books never mention. Whatever you take off drops to the foot, so you can put it back.
