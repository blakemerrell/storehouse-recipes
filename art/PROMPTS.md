# Section art — how the engravings were made, and the four still missing

Twelve of the sixteen sections open on a full-page engraving. Four do not:
**Snacks**, **Dinner**, **Power Drinks** and **Made, Not Bought**. Three of
those were created when *Run and Not Be Weary* was re-sectioned by meal and had
no art to inherit; the fourth never had any.

That leaves 70 recipes behind a plain heading — 61 of Volume One's 111 — while
the sections either side of them open on a picture. In a printed book that
reads as unfinished rather than as a decision.

The prompts that produced the existing set were never written down, which is
why this file exists. They cannot be regenerated from a cloud session: image
generation is not available there, and the vector samples in `art/svg/` are
explicitly not equivalent — clean contour line art against dense crosshatch.
So the four below are for running on a local machine.

---

## The house style

Read off the four Volume One engravings, which are the newest and cleanest of
the set — no burned-in caption band, so nothing has to be cropped off.

| | |
|---|---|
| Source size | **2400 × 1792** (4:3 landscape) |
| Colour | none — black ink on off-white |
| Technique | dense crosshatch and parallel-line hatching, steel engraving / woodcut |
| Composition | still life, 4–6 objects of varied height, read left to right |
| Ground | a kitchen counter or table, with a horizon line behind it |
| Text | **none**, anywhere in the image |
| Border | **none** — a drawn frame on one of the existing four is the odd one out, not the pattern |

Subjects are the actual food in the section, drawn specifically enough to be
recognised: an apple is that apple, a can is a can with its lid bent back. The
existing engravings are legible as *the storehouse order*, and that is most of
what they are doing on the page.

## The shared preamble

Every prompt below starts with this. It is repeated in each so they can be run
one at a time without scrolling back.

> A detailed black-and-white engraving in the style of a 19th-century steel
> engraving or woodcut illustration. Dense crosshatching and fine parallel-line
> hatching for all shading and shadow. Black ink on a warm off-white paper
> ground with a faint paper grain. No colour. No text, no lettering, no labels,
> no signage of any kind anywhere in the image. No border or frame. Landscape
> format, 4:3. A still life arranged on a plain kitchen counter, objects of
> varied height reading left to right, with a simple horizon line behind them.
> Everyday, unstyled home cooking — nothing restaurant-plated.

## 4 — Made, Not Bought

9 recipes. The section is named for what it produces, not what it takes in, and
the picture should be too: the shelf you filled yourself. Salt is in eight of
them, butter in five, flour in four.

The hard part is that four of the nine are liquids in jars and would draw
identically. Give each a different vessel and a different state — poured,
resting, mid-work.

> A detailed black-and-white engraving in the style of a 19th-century steel
> engraving or woodcut illustration. Dense crosshatching and fine parallel-line
> hatching for all shading and shadow. Black ink on a warm off-white paper
> ground with a faint paper grain. No colour. No text, no lettering, no labels,
> no signage of any kind anywhere in the image. No border or frame. Landscape
> format, 4:3. A still life arranged on a plain kitchen counter, objects of
> varied height reading left to right, with a simple horizon line behind them.
> Everyday, unstyled home cooking — nothing restaurant-plated.
>
> The subject: pantry staples made at home rather than bought. A tall glass bottle

## After the images exist

1. Save the four PNGs into `art/src/` under exactly the filenames above. The
   slug is derived from the section name — lowercase, `&` becomes `and`,
   everything else non-alphanumeric becomes a hyphen — so the name has to match
   or `secArt()` will return nothing and the section will silently keep its
   plain heading.
2. `node tools/prep-art.js` — strips any caption band, converts to grayscale
   line art on the page's own paper colour, sizes for half-letter at 300 dpi,
   and rewrites `data/art.js`.
3. `node tools/prep-art.js --preview` first if you want a contact sheet to
   judge them side by side against the existing twelve before committing.
4. Add the four new paths to `EXTRAS` in `sw.js` so they are cached offline,
   and bump `?v=` / the cache name.
5. `npm run build && npm run print` — each new opener adds about one page to
   its volume, and the READY_MADE page counts and the landing page are stamped
   automatically.
6. `node tests/run.js` — `tests/print.test.js` already asserts that every
   illustrated section opens on a page of its own and that the image actually
   loaded, so a wrong path fails rather than laying out at the right size with
   nothing in it.

## If a fifth section is ever added

Write its prompt here at the same time. The reason this file exists is that the
first twelve prompts were not kept, so the four that came later could not be
made to match by anything except guesswork off the finished images.
